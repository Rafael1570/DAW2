Carrito.php : 29

Represents a customer's shopping cart in an online store.

The cart lines are stored using the product SKU as the key, keeping its price and quantity. The total amounts are calculated by applying the discount first and then the VAT.

public total([float $descuento = 0.0 ]) : float
